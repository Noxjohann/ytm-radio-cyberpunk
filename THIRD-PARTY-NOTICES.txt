YTM Radio: credits and third-party components
=============================================

Based on Spot On Radio by Sz4bcsi (SZ4): the MP3 stream server and the RadioExt keep-alive / song-widget logic.
See LICENSE-SpotOnRadio.txt.

ytm-radio.exe statically links:
  shine-rs 0.1.5   LGPL-2.0   https://github.com/wshon/shine-rs  (pure-Rust MP3 encoder, port of Shine)
  wry, tao         MIT OR Apache-2.0  (Tauri project: window and WebView2)
  serde_json, base64, log   MIT OR Apache-2.0
(plus their transitive dependencies; run `cargo metadata` in the source for the full list)

LGPL notice (shine-rs): the complete corresponding source of this program, including Cargo.toml, is in the
repository this file ships from. You may modify shine-rs (or replace it with another version) and rebuild
with `cargo build --release`.

Required, installed separately: Cyber Engine Tweaks and RadioExt (by keanuWheeze). RED4ext is not needed.
Nothing of RadioExt is included or modified; the CET script only calls it at run time.
YTM Radio shows the YouTube Music web player in its own window; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Google, YouTube or CD PROJEKT RED.
